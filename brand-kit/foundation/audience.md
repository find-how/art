# Audience

## Primary Audience

- Laravel developers who want TypeScript and edge deployment without losing framework ergonomics.
- TypeScript developers tired of wiring infrastructure by hand.
- founders and agencies building production apps quickly on Cloudflare.
- Cloudflare-native teams that want a framework layer above Workers, D1, KV, R2, Queues, and Durable Objects.

## Buyer and User Motivation

They want production-shaped apps without assembling every primitive manually. They care about routes, jobs, auth, persistence, caching, logs, and local feedback. They do not want to become a platform team before shipping a product.

## Developer Fear

"This looks like magic and will break when I need real infrastructure."

## Brand Response

Show the mapping. Routes become Workers. DB calls become D1. Queue dispatches become background jobs. Wrangler returns a real response.
