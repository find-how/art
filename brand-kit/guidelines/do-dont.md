# Do / Don't

## Do

Say:

> Pioneer turns business logic into Cloudflare-native infrastructure.

Say:

> Routes become Workers. DB calls become D1 bindings. Queue dispatches become background jobs.

Say:

> Ask AI for the endpoint. Pioneer makes it runnable.

Say:

> Write the checkout logic. Pioneer wires the app.

## Don't

Do not lead with:

> Pioneer is a Rust compiler with nominal module identity and compile-time DI.

Do not lead with:

> Pioneer is Laravel for TypeScript.

Do not lead with:

> Pioneer is an AI framework.

The public opener is the running app story. The architecture comes after.
