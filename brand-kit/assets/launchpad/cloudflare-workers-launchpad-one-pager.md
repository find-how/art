# Pioneer Cloudflare Workers Launchpad One-Pager

## One-Liner

Pioneer is the full-stack TypeScript framework that turns business logic into production-ready Cloudflare edge infrastructure.

## Launchpad Framing

Cloudflare has the primitives. Pioneer gives developers the framework layer: routes, database calls, cache work, queue jobs, auth, logs, local feedback, and a deploy path from one TypeScript application surface.

## Audience

- Laravel developers moving to TypeScript and the edge.
- TypeScript teams tired of wiring infrastructure by hand.
- founders and agencies shipping production apps quickly on Cloudflare.
- Cloudflare-native teams that want a framework above Workers, D1, KV, R2, Queues, and Durable Objects.

## Demo Proof

Ask AI for a checkout endpoint. Pioneer generates `app/routes/web.ts`, infers the Worker route, D1 table, edge cache, queue job, auth middleware, logs, and Wrangler local smoke request.

## Do Not Lead With

Do not lead with compiler architecture, DI, TypeGraph, or formal guarantees. Those prove the moat after the product story lands.
